Smoothstep

Regularized sign function and tipping geometry

Parameters

x₀ = 1/m − w/2
w = 2(θ₀ − 1/m)
1/m < θ₀ < 2/m

The contact slider is limited to ±90°. The orange tangent shows the local slope tan(θ); after θ₀ the sides continue vertically. The dashed centreline passes through the object centre.

Geometric interpretation

r(θ) = r₀ sgn(θ)
dy/dr = tan(θ)
dy/dθ = r₀ sgn′(θ) tan(θ)

The plotted curve is mirrored about the origin. Once sgn′ becomes zero, r stays at ±r₀ and the geometric path becomes vertical.
