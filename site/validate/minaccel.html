Target θ (deg)

The base acceleration ramps up linearly (ay = 0, so the tipping direction stays φ = 0). The solve runs until |θ| first reaches the target angle; the acceleration at that moment is the minimum needed to rock the body that far. Once the target is reached the load is removed and the simulation continues for 10 more seconds to show the free response.

Since it is a linear model (that works on small angle approximation), disabling BW force yields results that are inaccurate at higher θ.
