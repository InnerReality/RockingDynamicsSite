Chapter 3 · Free vibration

Rocking after the forcing stops

Once the base acceleration returns to zero, the body does not immediately settle. It trades potential and rotational kinetic energy while impacts at the alternating corners remove a portion of that energy.

Impacts as dissipation

At each pivot switch, the velocity field changes abruptly. A coefficient of restitution models the fraction of rotational energy retained by the post-impact motion. Lower restitution means fewer visible rocking cycles.
