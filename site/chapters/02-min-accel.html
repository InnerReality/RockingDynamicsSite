Chapter 2 · Tipping threshold

How much acceleration starts the rocking?

Before a body rocks, its contact forces can redistribute while both corners remain on the base. Rocking begins when the reaction at one corner reaches zero and the body is free to pivot about the other.

A geometric threshold

In the quasi-static limit, the critical horizontal acceleration follows from the line of action of the effective gravity vector. The result depends on the center-of-mass geometry; mass cancels from the balance of moments.

Experiment. Ramp the base acceleration slowly, note the first lift-off, and compare it with the threshold predicted by the body’s aspect ratio.

What to notice

The threshold is a geometry statement, not a mass statement. Changing the body’s weight changes the forces but not the acceleration at which the moment balance first becomes unstable.

The threshold is the bridge between the static geometry and the dynamic response in the next chapter.
